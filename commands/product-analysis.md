description: "Generate an objective product analysis — features, pricing, competitors, differentiators, pain points, and market capture"
argument-hint: "<product-name>"
---

Use the `product-analyst` agent to research and analyze the given product.

The agent will:

1. Conduct multiple web searches to gather current information about the product
2. Research features, pricing, competitors, and market position
3. Surface real user frustrations from reviews, forums, and social media
4. Synthesize everything into a structured, objective analysis document

The document is saved to `docs/product-analysis/<product-name>.md` and covers:

- Executive Summary
- Features
- Differentiators
- Pricing Model
- Competitors
- Pain Points & Common Frustrations
- Market Capture

The agent prioritizes objectivity — it presents balanced analysis with both strengths and weaknesses, sourced from real data.
