Use the `mattpocock-skills:grilling` skill to interview me about the topic in the arguments. If
no argument is given, grill me about whatever this conversation has been about.

This plugin has no grilling skill of its own. It deleted it on 2026-09-24 in favor of this one, so
`/grill-me` needs `mattpocock-skills` installed.

Ask questions. Do not write code, plans, or beads during the interview.
